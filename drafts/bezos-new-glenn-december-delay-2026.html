Jeff Bezos on New Glenn’s return to flight

Said · October 7, 2026

“December of this year, we're going to fly that vehicle again.”

Fox News Channel’s Special Report with Bret Baier · Bezos discussing Blue Origin’s New Glenn rocket, as quoted by Reuters

The Reality · November 5, 2026

Blue Origin moved New Glenn’s next launch to January 2027, according to two people familiar with the plan and an internal CEO memo reviewed by Reuters. CNBC also reviewed the memo and reported that the revised target was about ten days later than the previous one.

The December return-to-flight target moved into January before December even arrived.

Reuters’ November 5 report directly revises the December 2026 New Glenn flight date Bezos gave on October 7. This is a changed schedule, not proof that a December flight actually failed: the month has not yet arrived, and a January target is not a completed launch. CEO Dave Limp attributed the slippage to launchpad reconstruction and summer weather after a May test explosion. Bezos made the forecast as Blue Origin’s founder, but the company operates the rocket. Forbes independently estimates his personal fortune well above $100 million.
