Jack Dorsey on Web5

Said · June 10, 2022

“this will likely be our most important contribution to the internet.”

Dorsey on X, introducing Block’s TBD Web5 project

The Reality · November 7, 2024

Block told shareholders it was “winding down TBD”, the unit behind Web5, to invest instead in bitcoin mining and its Bitkey wallet.

The “most important contribution” became an initiative to wind down.

Dorsey said “likely,” not guaranteed. This was a decision about Block’s Web5 initiative, not an end to all decentralized-web work or to Block’s other bitcoin products.
