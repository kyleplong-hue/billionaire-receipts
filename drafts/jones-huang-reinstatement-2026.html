Harvey Jones on Nvidia’s CEO

Said · November 17, 2026

“The NVIDIA Board unanimously accepted his resignation and does not intend to reinstate Jensen as CEO.”

Nvidia’s third-quarter earnings call · Jones speaking as board chair, according to the company-hosted transcript

The Reality · November 23, 2026

Six days later, Nvidia’s board unanimously reinstated Jensen Huang as president, CEO and a director, effective immediately. In the company’s November 23 announcement, Jones said the board now believed Huang was “the right person to lead NVIDIA.”

“Does not intend to reinstate” lasted six days. Jones remained chair when the board voted to bring him back.

The November 23 board vote directly reverses the position Jones stated on its behalf during the November 17 earnings call. “Does not intend” described the board’s intention then, not an irrevocable pledge; circumstances can change. Nvidia said discussions with Huang and input from senior U.S. officials informed its new decision. The board says its independent investigation will continue, so reinstatement is not a finding that all questions have been resolved. Forbes independently estimates Jones’s own fortune at about $1.6 billion; the decision belongs to Nvidia’s board, not Jones acting alone.
