Donald Trump on ending inflation

Said · August 9, 2024

“Starting on day one, we will end inflation and make America affordable again, to bring down the prices of all goods.”

Campaign rally in Bozeman, Montana · Trump describing what he would do after returning to the White House

The Reality · November 10, 2026

The Bureau of Labor Statistics reported that U.S. consumer prices rose 0.4% in October 2026, following a 0.6% rise in September. Food rose 0.5% in October and the index excluding food and energy rose 0.3%. Reuters reported that the headline monthly increase exceeded the 0.3% economists had expected.

The day-one promise to end inflation met another month of rising prices.

The November 10 CPI release directly tests Trump’s August 2024 promise about inflation: the broad price index was still increasing well after his return to office. This is a comparison of his political pledge with a national economic measure, not proof that one person caused every price change. Inflation means the rate of price increase, not the level of prices; some individual prices fell. Because the 2025 shutdown prevented October price collection, BLS did not publish an official October 2026 year-over-year rate. Forbes independently estimates Trump’s personal fortune above $100 million.
