Jared Kushner on the pandemic’s trajectory

Said · April 29, 2020

“We’re on the other side of the medical aspect of this.”

Fox & Friends interview · discussing the U.S. COVID-19 response and reopening

The Reality · July 16, 2020

The CDC’s July 16 update counted 67,404 new U.S. cases and 947 new deaths, bringing its totals to 3,483,832 cases and 136,938 deaths. A day later, CIDRAP reported a new daily case record of more than 75,000.

The country was not on the other side of the medical crisis.

In the same interview, Kushner said he thought much of the country should be back to normal by June and hoped it would be “really rocking again” by July. That was optimism, not a guaranteed forecast. The case counts and deaths show the crisis persisted; they do not by themselves assign personal responsibility for it to Kushner.
