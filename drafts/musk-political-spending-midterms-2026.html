Elon Musk on political spending

Said · May 20, 2025

“I think in terms of political spending I’m going to do a lot less in the future.”

Bloomberg’s Mishal Husain interviewing Musk by video at the Qatar Economic Forum, when asked specifically about spending on the upcoming midterms; Bloomberg published the transcript May 21.

The Reality · November 18, 2026

OpenSecrets counted $445,719,690 in Musk’s 2026-cycle political contributions, up from more than $291 million it counted for 2024. At the November 18 DealBook Summit, Musk said he felt “pretty foolish” about spending so much on the midterms and seeing little return: “Probably, if I could rewind the clock, I would have spent less money.”

He said “a lot less.” The next cycle’s tally was a lot more—and he now says he would spend less if he could do it again.

His November 18 admission about the very midterms he was asked about in 2025 is the fresh development connecting the promise to his personal donations. Musk had qualified the earlier remark: “If I see a reason to do political spending in the future, I’ll do it.” The comparison is between OpenSecrets’ cycle totals, not a claim that he promised zero donations or that spending alone determined election results. Bloomberg’s transcript is lightly edited for clarity; the video lets readers check the exchange.
