Sam Altman on ads in AI

Said · May 1, 2024

“I kind of think of ads as like a last resort for us for a business model.”

Harvard fireside chat · in response to a question about advertising as a way to fund access to AI

The Reality · October 21, 2026

OpenAI said more than 100,000 advertisers had run ChatGPT campaigns and expanded ads to 89 countries. In August, it had reported a $1 billion annualized ad-revenue run rate.

The “last resort” became a global business line.

Altman said he was not categorically against ads and would consider them to widen access; he did not promise never to run them. OpenAI says the ads are labeled, separate from answers and restricted to Free and Go plans. The revenue figure is an annualized run rate, not revenue actually earned over a year. This was OpenAI’s business decision, not Altman’s personal ad sales.
