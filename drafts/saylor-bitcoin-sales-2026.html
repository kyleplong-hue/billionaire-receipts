Michael Saylor on Strategy’s bitcoin

Said · October 17, 2024

“We’re going to buy Bitcoin, never sell Bitcoin, we’re going to borrow money intelligently.”

Markets with Madison interview · describing MicroStrategy’s approach to investors

The Reality · July 6, 2026

Strategy disclosed selling 1,363 bitcoin on June 29–30 and 2,225 more on July 1–5. It said the proceeds funded preferred-stock distributions and replenished its dollar reserve.

“Never sell” met a dividend bill.

Saylor was describing the company’s strategy, not promising what he personally would do with his own coins. The company—not Saylor personally—made these sales, and it still held 843,775 bitcoin on July 5. It had sold bitcoin before, in December 2022; these were not its first-ever sales.
