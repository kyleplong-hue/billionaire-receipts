Donald Trump on the 2026 midterms

Said · February 20, 2025

“I think we’re going to really increase our margins by a lot.”

Republican Governors Association meeting · discussing the party’s prospects in the 2026 midterms

The Reality · November 4, 2026

In the November 3 midterms, Democrats won control of both the House and Senate. They flipped seven Republican-held Senate seats and more than two dozen House seats, according to November 4 reporting.

The margins grew—for the other party.

The November 4 election results directly test Trump’s 2025 forecast of larger Republican margins in these midterms. It was a prediction (“I think”), not a guarantee or a claim that Trump himself was on the ballot. Some individual races and final chamber margins were still unsettled when these results were reported; the change in control of both chambers was clear. Forbes independently estimated his personal fortune at $7 billion in September 2026.
