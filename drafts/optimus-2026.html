Elon Musk on Optimus

Said · April 23, 2024

“And then I think we may be able to sell it externally by the end of next year. These are just guesses.”

Tesla Q1 earnings call · “next year” meant 2025

The Reality · October 21, 2026

Musk now expects Optimus deliveries to companies outside Tesla around the end of 2027. He says the robot’s AI, hardware and reliability still need work.

The robot has a production line. The delivery date got two more years.

His 2024 date was explicitly a guess, not a guaranteed launch. Tesla says it has started early production; the new date is also a forecast, not a completed delivery.
