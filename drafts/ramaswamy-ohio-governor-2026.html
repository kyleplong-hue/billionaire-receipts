Vivek Ramaswamy on Ohio’s governor election

Said · October 27, 2026

“The good news is that one week from today, we are going to win this election in a decisive victory.”

Rally in Hebron, Ohio · quoted by 10TV while urging supporters to vote

The Reality · November 5, 2026

Ohio’s election ended with Amy Acton defeating Ramaswamy by nearly eight percentage points, according to the Statehouse News Bureau’s November 5 report. He conceded the race; MultiState’s November 6 tally showed Acton at 52.3% and Ramaswamy at 44.4%.

His decisive victory went to his opponent.

The November 5 report on the November 3 result directly tests his October 27 prediction about this very election. It was campaign confidence, not a guaranteed result; polling already showed Acton ahead at the time. The vote totals shown above were still unofficial and may change, but the race had been called and Ramaswamy had conceded. Forbes estimated his own fortune at $2.3 billion in September.
