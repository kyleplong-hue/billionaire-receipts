Changpeng Zhao on safeguarding crypto

Said · November 25, 2022

“We believe that our wallet infrastructure is one of the most secure in the industry and that Binance is the best place to safeguard crypto.”

Binance blog · Zhao, then its CEO, explaining how its own wallets protected assets

The Reality · November 2, 2026

After the October 29 breach, Binance put the estimated impact at about $6.3 billion and temporarily suspended withdrawals. November 2 reporting counted $3.64 billion in customer withdrawals after service resumed; Binance said it had replaced 95% of the stolen assets.

The “best place to safeguard crypto” had a $6.3 billion breach to repair.

Zhao left the CEO job in 2023; this was a claim he made while leading Binance, not a claim he personally operated its wallets in 2026. “Best” was a comparative security opinion, not a no-hack guarantee. Binance says it will cover losses from its own funds and its SAFU reserve; the theft figure is not a claim that customers ultimately lost $6.3 billion. The attack’s root cause remained under investigation in Binance’s initial notice.
