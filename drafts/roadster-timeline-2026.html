Elon Musk on the Roadster

Said · February 28, 2024

“Production design complete and unveil end of year”

Musk on X · from a thread that also aimed to ship the car the following year, 2025

The Reality · October 15, 2026

Tesla unveiled a redesigned Roadster in October 2026. Musk said production would start in 2028—not an unveiling in 2024 or shipments in 2025.

The reveal arrived two years after the forecast. The cars have another two years to wait.

“Aiming” was a target, not a guarantee. Tesla demonstrated a vehicle in 2026 and is still taking reservations; the 2028 production date is a new forecast, not proof of deliveries. The Roadster was first shown in 2017, when Musk expected it to be available in 2020.
