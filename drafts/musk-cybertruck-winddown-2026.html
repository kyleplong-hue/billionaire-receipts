Elon Musk on Cybertruck production

Said · October 31, 2023

“We’re aiming to make about 200,000 a year at volume production.”

Joe Rogan Experience #2054 · Musk answering how many Cybertrucks Tesla could make at scale

The Reality · November 9, 2026

Musk said Tesla would shut down the Texas Cybertruck line in about eight months and refund deposits for many unfilled orders. His November 6 announcement had already set a mid-2027 wind-down to make room for Cybercab production. Kelley Blue Book estimated just 20,237 U.S. Cybertruck sales in 2025, down from 38,965 in 2024.

The 200,000-a-year ambition now has a shutdown timetable at its Texas factory.

The November 6–9 wind-down, extra-shift and refund announcements directly test Musk’s forecast for this same truck. “Aiming” was a production goal, not a guarantee or a dated promise; the sales estimates are U.S. sales, not a measure of worldwide factory output. Musk now says outstanding orders exceed what the line can build before it closes, so the 2025 sales shortfall should not be read as a current absence of orders. The announced shutdown is for the Texas line; Reuters said it was not yet clear whether Cybertruck production would move elsewhere or end altogether. Forbes independently estimates Musk’s personal fortune in the hundreds of billions; Tesla’s production decision is the company’s, not his personal vehicle output.
