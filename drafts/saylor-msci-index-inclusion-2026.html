Michael Saylor on index inclusion

Said · May 1, 2025

“MSTR inclusion in QQQ, MSCI, and Crypto Indices drives superior passive capital flows.”

Strategy’s Q1 2025 earnings presentation, slide 62, in the section introduced as “Michael Saylor, Executive Chairman.” This is text on a company slide, not a verified spoken quotation.

The Reality · November 11, 2026

Asked about possible MSCI exclusion in December 2025, Saylor told Reuters: “It won’t make any difference, in my opinion.” On November 11, MSCI announced it would remove Strategy from the MSCI USA and MSCI World indexes, effective December 1.

Index inclusion drove “superior passive capital flows” in the pitch. Facing exclusion from MSCI, Saylor said losing it would make no difference.

The slide praised QQQ, MSCI and crypto indexes together; the later comment concerned MSCI alone. The decision is real, but the removal and any resulting fund outflows have not yet happened. This is a shift in how Saylor framed the value of inclusion, not proof that his forecast of no financial impact was false.
