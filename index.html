Elon MuskMoney & power

“In terms of political spending, I’m going to do a lot less in the future. I think I’ve done enough.”

May 2025 · Qatar Economic Forum
The receipt · October 2026

Last week’s campaign filings disclosed $158 million he gave to a Senate Republican super PAC in September. Reuters puts his spending this midterm cycle at roughly $366 million.

We all think he’s done quite enough. Apparently, he couldn’t help himself.

SpaceX’s IPO made him the world’s first trillionaire in June. The money is enormous to everyone else. The power looks harder to give up.
