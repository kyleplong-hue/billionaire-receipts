Mark ZuckerbergPrivacy
May 24, 2010 · Washington Post

“You have control over how your information is shared.”

What happened · October 21, 2026

A New Mexico judge ordered Meta to pay $25 billion after a jury found the company misled users about their data. The jury specifically found the 2010 user-control statement deceptive.

The control was in the promise. The jury found it wasn't in users' hands.

This is a judgment about years of earlier conduct, not a new data breach. Meta disputes the ruling and says it will appeal.
